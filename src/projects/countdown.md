# Every Moment Counted Beatily 
[Link to the countdowns](https://countdown.kervian.com)

Countdown is a living calendar of the moments worth waiting for. Every holiday gets its own full-screen timer and its own animated canvas — fireworks on New Year's, snowfall at Christmas, lanterns rising for the Lunar New Year.

Open any holiday to watch it tick down, switch between its hand-made canvases, or build a countdown of your own — a birthday, a launch, a trip — and share it with a link that carries every setting.

And it keeps itself honest: every celebration rolls forward to its next date automatically, year after year.

- 40+ holidays across religion, culture, seasons & fun
- A dozen+ animated canvases, including emojis
- Custom countdowns with shareable links
